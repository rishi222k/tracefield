# Sources and asset provenance

Tracefield ships no demo images or third-party reference screenshots. Unit tests use original synthetic geometry and pixels.

- shadcn/ui source components: https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui — MIT; license in `licenses/shadcn-MIT.txt`.
- Fonts: Roboto Mono, Courier Prime and Inter from https://github.com/google/fonts, under SIL Open Font License; notices in `public/fonts/`. Geist is bundled through `@fontsource-variable/geist`; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Visual-language reading: Jorge Almeida's fictional interface work, https://jorgeonline.me/Iron-Man-2; no imagery is copied from that page.

Future demo images must record the exact source URL, creator, applicable license and any modifications before they are committed. The code's MIT license does not grant rights to user-provided images.
